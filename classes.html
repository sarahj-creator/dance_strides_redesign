Classes

The Primus Technique and Foundation Training are woven into our Modern and Modern Jazz classes. Contact the studio for the current timetable.

For Young Dancers

Preparatory & Acro Modern

3 to 9 years

Pupils learn the basics of dance and coordination, working in couples or groups with props like balls, streamers, and feathers. As they progress, they're introduced to the basics of acro dance and more advanced movement sequences.

Boys' Technique

5 years and up

Young men are taught the basics of dance alongside elements of Street Jazz, with an emphasis on strength, flexibility, and discipline.

For Teens & Adults

Modern (Adults)

Based on the Horton technique.

Modern Jazz (10 to 18 years)

Horton technique based material and jazz dance idioms.

Afro/Caribbean (Children & Adults)

Traditional African and Caribbean dance forms.

Ballet (Children & Adults)

Free-style, with influences from the Cecchetti and Vaganova techniques.

Jazzercise (Adults)

A cardio and strength-training class.

Jazz (Teens & Adults)

Lyrical and music-video styles.

Foundation Training & Private Sessions

Ms. Trotman, a certified Level 1 Foundation Training instructor, offers personalised day-time rehabilitation sessions and private dance classes. Contact the studio for details.
