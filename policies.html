School Policies

Every dancer must have a waiver on file at the office. Dancers 17 and under must be signed for by a parent or legal guardian.

Attendance & Conduct

  1. 1. The school has the right to determine the suitability of students for particular classes, and may require a student to change classes based on ability or progress.
  2. 2. Dancers will not be permitted into a class after ten minutes of commencement, unless given permission by the instructor, though they may observe.
  3. 3. Any dancer missing more than two consecutive classes without explanation will be required to present a letter from a parent or guardian, and to catch up with fellow students outside of class time.
  4. 4. The school has the right to suspend any dancer who does not show full and consistent commitment to their training, until the matter is addressed with a parent/guardian and the Principal or Assistant Principal.
  5. 5. Ill-discipline will not be accepted, and will be addressed by the Principal or Assistant Principal.

Studio Attire & Etiquette

Leotards and tights/leggings, ballet slippers for Ballet, and jazz shoes for Jazz classes.

No jewelry, hats, or bandannas allowed in class.

Hairstyles must be neat, tidy, and not a hindrance during class.

No street shoes are allowed in the studio.

No eating or drinking in the studio, and no eating within thirty minutes of a class starting.

Watching classes is not permitted unless the tutor or person in charge gives permission.

No games or toys are allowed on the premises.

Cellular telephones may not be used in the studio.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

  • We identify our purposes for collecting personal information before or at the time it is collected.
  • We use personal information only for the purposes stated, unless we have consent or are required by law to do otherwise.
  • We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil those purposes.
  • We collect personal information by fair and lawful means, with the knowledge or consent of the individual where appropriate.
  • We keep personal information accurate, complete, and up to date, relevant to the purposes it is used for.
  • We protect personal information with security safeguards against loss, theft, unauthorised access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification.
  • We make our policies and procedures for managing personal information available to customers on request.
