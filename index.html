A Bajan institution, one dancer at a time.

Dance Strides Barbados was established by M. Sheron Trotman in April 1994. Recognised by a National Task Force on Cultural Industries as one of the island's major dance companies, the school has trained generations of dancers across Ballet, Modern, Jazz, Afro/Caribbean, Hip Hop, Acrodance and more.

Every year the school stages a professional concert and a children's showcase, and runs an inter-class choreography competition for teens, often with an international guest choreographer or tutor.

The Primus legacy

Our Modern classes carry on the technique of the late Dr. Pearl E. Primus, developed further by founder M. Sheron Trotman. Teens and adults also train in Foundation Training, a layered set of exercises that decompresses the spine, opens the chest, and builds a strong core.
