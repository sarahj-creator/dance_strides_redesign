What happens every year

Annual Concert

A professional dance concert showcasing every discipline the school teaches.

Children's Showcase

A dedicated stage moment for the school's youngest dancers and their families.

Teen Choreography Competition

An inter-class competition that gives teenagers a chance to choreograph and lead.

When schedules allow, the school also brings in an international choreographer, dancer, or tutor for a guest workshop. Exact dates for the current term are shared on Instagram and Facebook, and by contacting the studio directly.
