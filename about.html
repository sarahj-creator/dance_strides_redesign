About Dance Strides

A registered educational institution, established with a single roll of 100 students and grown into one of Barbados's major dance companies.

Founded by M. Sheron Trotman

Dance Strides Barbados was established by M. Sheron Trotman in April 1994. The school has been listed by a National Task Force on Cultural Industries as one of the island's major dance companies, and today trains students from age 3 through adult.

The school provides instruction across Preparatory and Creative Modern, Boys' Technique, Hip Hop, Acrodance, Modern (including the Primus Technique), Jazz, Afro/Caribbean and Ballet, alongside Mime and Drama workshops. Fitness-oriented classes are also offered, including Jazzercise, Yoga, and Foundation Training.

A school that performs

Every year the school engages in a major project: a professional dance concert, a children's showcasing, and an inter-class choreography competition for teenagers, often with the attachment of an international choreographer, dancer, or tutor.

Dance Strides has also participated in intensive summer dance workshops at Howard University in Washington, DC, where founder M. Sheron Trotman was a regular contributor, teaching the Pearl Primus Technique among other genres.

Come dance with us.

New students of all ages are welcome throughout the term.
